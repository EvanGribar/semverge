# Flagship homepage direction

The homepage is a Persuade surface for developers evaluating the open-source release engine and the direction of the project. The existing operations workspace remains a separate Operate surface.

## Direction

- Product: a release engine evolving toward connected operational context.
- Character: exact, restrained, investigative.
- World: change records and operational instruments under directional silver light.
- Moments: the converging planes in the hero and a readable evidence trail.
- Signature: the convergence mark and a record that places release evidence beside an incident.

Three concepts were considered: an editorial release ledger (legible but potentially austere), a sculptural convergence scene (distinctive but abstract), and a connected evidence trail (specific to the product and able to carry both clarity and visual character). The last was selected. Alternatives to the headline included “Keep the context after release” and “From release to response”; “Software doesn’t stop at deploy” gives the clearest reason to look beyond the release engine, with the supporting copy stating the actual availability boundary.

The illustrative scene is not a screenshot or production data. It expresses the relationship available in the workspace pull request: HTTP checks and incidents can be viewed in service context, and an operator can associate release evidence. The relationship does not establish causation. The still was authored with Stageshow's React `Stage` and `Window` kit, based on its `dark-hero` example, and rendered in separate wide and compact crops. The published homepage serves those stills without a JavaScript or React runtime. See [the rehearsal](stageshow/REHEARSAL.md) for reproduction and limits. The exploratory mark and locally hosted Manrope font were selectively brought from platform branch commit `8776f132ba4353339a7122e2e929c44a6fcd7d89`; Manrope's license is in `manrope-OFL.txt`. The mark is an original exploratory asset, not an approved final identity.

The visual research considered Sentry's expressive headline, Better Stack's product-led hero, Linear's restrained typography and real interface, and Vercel's singular first-screen visual. None of their assets or copy are used. The Sero reference provided the monochrome material direction.

Unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir), CC BY 4.0, informed the spacing, color, copy, type, layout, details, visual and motion review. Impeccable's shape, bolder, critique, audit and polish guidance informed the direction and verification. Stageshow by Benji Taylor supplied the product-illustration kit; its MIT license is included alongside the scene source.
