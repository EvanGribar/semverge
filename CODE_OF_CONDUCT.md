# Code of Conduct

SemVerge is a community project. We want participation to be welcoming, useful, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while addressing the technical substance.
- Give and receive specific, constructive feedback.
- Keep public discussions focused on the project and protect private information.
- Make space for quieter contributors and welcome questions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish credentials, private conversations, or other sensitive information.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub rather than opening a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other action needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
